## 1. Audit Scope

Only `docs/architecture/AKIRA-TARGET-ARCHITECTURE.md` was audited, and the document was treated as its own source of truth.

- No repository implementation was inspected.
- No comparison was made against the current code, the Phase 0 disposition or the architecture audit.
- No architectural decision was made, changed or reopened.
- The audited document was not modified.

Section numbers below are the audited document's own (§1–§23). "Dn", "On" and "Xn" are its decision, open-question and contradiction IDs.

---

## 2. Method

1. **Inventory.** I read the whole document. Every statement labelled DECISION, TARGET, PROPOSED or OPEN was recorded, together with unlabelled statements that clearly commit to something (diagram captions, table cells under a labelled column, and the §23 principles). For each I noted the section, the commitment and its dependencies. This covered about 140 labelled statements, the 9 closed decisions (D1–D8, D12), 12 open-question entries (O1–O12, with O5 closed and O7r and O8a as sub-IDs), 15 contradiction and refinement entries (X1–X21, not contiguous), and 19 principles.
2. **Forward check.** Each commitment was compared against every later section, using the eleven tests in the audit brief: contradiction, competing owner, reassigned authority, closed reopened, open treated as decided, terminology drift, a different target, PROPOSED sounding decided, DECISION sounding proposed, ownership or boundary ambiguity, and shorthand. The focus areas named in the brief got particular attention.
3. **Mechanical reference check.** A script checked every `§n` cross-reference against the document's headings, every "principle n" reference against §23, and every O/X/D identifier against its definition. Unmatched `§` targets were then confirmed to be Phase 0 (`P0 §…`) citations.
4. **Known intentions** (brief §4–§5) were applied as constraints. The ownership-vs-placement split for the Capability Registry, O3, O7, New Chat vs Reset, the runtime principle vs placement, model transport principle vs placement, and the MCP scope were not flagged merely because they are open.

## 7. Final Verdict

### PASS WITH MINOR WORDING ISSUES

No architectural contradictions were found. No later section changes an owner, reassigns authority, reopens a closed decision or settles an open one. The ten findings (C1–C10) are wording and cross-reference issues. Each can be fixed by rewording in place without changing any DECISION, TARGET, PROPOSED or OPEN status. Fixing them is optional and is not done by this audit.

Phase 1 target architecture is internally consistent based on this document-only audit. No architectural decision should be reopened as part of this audit.
