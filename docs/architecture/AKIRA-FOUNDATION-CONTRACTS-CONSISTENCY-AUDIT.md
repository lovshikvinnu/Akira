## 1. Audit Scope

- I compared the contracts statement by statement against the TA and against the Phase 1 audit's final state (findings C1–C10, already applied to the TA).
- The TA is authoritative. Where the contracts and the TA differ, the contracts are treated as defective.
- No source code, tests, configuration or repository implementation was inspected.
- Every TA passage cited below was checked against the TA's current text. Where a finding depends on exact wording, the TA wording is quoted.

### Classification vocabulary

| Class                            | Meaning                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CONSISTENT                       | Restates the TA faithfully                                                                                                         |
| REFINEMENT                       | Adds precision that follows directly from the TA                                                                                   |
| NEW BUT SAFE                     | Adds detail the TA does not mention, without touching any owner, authority, lifecycle, persistence rule, topology or open question |
| NEW ARCHITECTURAL DECISION       | Adds or strengthens an architectural commitment the TA does not make                                                               |
| CONTRADICTION                    | Conflicts with a TA statement                                                                                                      |
| PREMATURELY CLOSED OPEN QUESTION | Settles, fully or partly, something the TA leaves OPEN                                                                             |
| AMBIGUOUS                        | Admits a reading that conflicts with the TA or leans on an open question                                                           |

Severity: **MAJOR** means two implementers could build different architectures. **MINOR** means local and correctable by rewording.

---

## 2. Verdict

### CONTRACT DEFECTS FOUND

The contracts are largely faithful to the TA:

- The ownership split for capabilities (owner, registration, activation, selection, routing, execution) is exact.
- The VAJRA, GENESIS and HANDS boundaries match D1, D2, D6 and D7.
- MCP matches D4 and D5.
- Every preserved open question remains open in substance.

The contracts are **not** yet a faithful operationalisation, for three reasons:

1. There are three contradictions with specific TA statements. One is MAJOR, because it reads as VAJRA routing non-mission reads.
2. There is one partial premature closure of O4.
3. There are six new architectural decisions that the TA does not authorise, several presented as CONTRACT rather than PROPOSED.

None of these requires reopening the TA. Every finding can be corrected inside the contracts document.
